# Working with Brett — Northeastern endowment analysis

## Branding (always, for Northeastern work)
- Colors: Northeastern Red #C8102E (primary accent, emphasis, "crimson"), black, white; Gold #A4804A as a minor accent only.
- Font: Lato. Clean white backgrounds, square corners, black rules. No beige, no serif display fonts, no rounded cards.
- Footer: source line left, "Northeastern University" right. The goal is that nothing looks AI-generated.

## Number formatting
- Headline dollars rounded to whole millions: $484M, not $484.0M. Tables may use one decimal ($M).
- Pattern: "287 (27.6%) of scholarship endowments hold 80% ($387M) of scholarship market value."
- Always show both count and dollars, each with % of total.

## Slides
- Leadership audience. Main slide: bullets plus visuals. Backup slides: clean spreadsheet-style tables.
- Concentration curves (largest to smallest, "Top N = X%" callout), not Pareto charts.
- Keep every label inside the chart area. No striped or gradient table backgrounds; plain white.
- After every publish, open the artifact for Brett rather than only posting a link.
- Check that every chart label fits before handing a slide over, and ask whether Brett wants a render check.

## Endowment data (Summary_ComprehensiveFundHistory.xlsx)
- The last row is a grand total: exclude it. FY26 (June 30, 2026): 1,799 funds, $2.302B market value; 8 funds at $0 are included in counts.
- Default measure: Market Value as of June 30.
- Scholarship = fund name matches scholar|schol|sch|schl|scho (word-boundary), excluding "Faculty Scholarship".
  Fellowships, prizes/awards, loans and stipends are excluded but noted as possible additions.
- FY26 reference: 1,039 scholarships = 57.8% of count, $484M = 21.0% of MV. Top 100 funds = 73.4% of MV.
  Top 100 scholarships = $277M (57% of scholarship MV, 12% of total). 287 scholarships = 80% of scholarship MV.
